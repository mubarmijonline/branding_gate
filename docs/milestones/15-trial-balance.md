# 15. Trial balance

**Goal.** Ahmed (2026-11-11): a trial balance by month, quarter, year or any
dates, grouped by account, with each account's opening balance first and its
closing balance last, as a tab in Finance Management.

**Acceptance check.**
- Trial Balance tab shows Opening (Dr/Cr), Period movement (Dr/Cr), Closing
  (Dr/Cr) for every account and group; totals balance.
- One period's closing equals the next period's opening; the whole history
  equals the Finance Tree (`tests/test_trial_balance.py`).
- Excel and print; English and Arabic.
